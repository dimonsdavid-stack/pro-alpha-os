Decision questions

Clarity before commitment.

A premium system should answer the hard questions before asking for a card.

Is this a consultation or an LLC filing service?

Neither. Pro Alpha OS is the complete, software-driven implementation system for an approved two-entity architecture. Qualification can support, hold, or decline that path; accepted files move through document assembly, payment, jurisdiction review, professional gates, filing reconciliation, and operating records as one delivery.

Can I buy a cheaper one-entity or document-only tier?

No. The $4,999 product is the full dual-entity system. Pro Alpha OS does not use a low-cost LLC as a lead-in and then reconstruct the real scope through add-ons.

What does automated mean if professionals still review filings?

Automation controls intake, document assembly, dependency routing, payment state, filing handoffs, status updates, and record delivery. Facts-specific legal and tax decisions remain behind human review gates. That is a control, not a reduction in the product.

Does forming the entities guarantee Trader Tax Status or a Section 475 election?

No. TTS depends on the actual trading facts, and Section 475 has eligibility, scope, and timing requirements. The system organizes the evidence and election workflow; it does not guarantee an IRS conclusion or cure a client-owned missed deadline.

Is Wyoming automatically the correct state for my trading entity?

No. Residence, where the business operates, state taxes, foreign qualification, recurring reports, and other facts can change the jurisdiction path. The filing state is cleared downstream and is not promised by the headline.

What happens after I qualify?

You receive a desk reference and one of three preliminary routes: architecture supported, professional review required, or architecture not supported. Only a supported path opens the complete commercial implementation flow.

What continues after year one?

The first operating year is included in the implementation. The operating OS renews at $999 per year after that. Optional annual compliance support is scoped separately at $1,499–$2,499 depending on the accepted file.
