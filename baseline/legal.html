Service scope

Pro Alpha OS is the Trading Business Infrastructure product presented by Pro Alpha Systems. It provides software-driven intake, architecture routing, document assembly, dependency control, deadline tracking, approved dual-entity formation coordination, filing-status reconciliation, and operating-record delivery.

The $4,999 year-one implementation is the complete commercial scope shown before checkout. It is not a consultation, single-entity filing, tax return, legal opinion, investment recommendation, or promise of a particular tax position. The operating OS renews at $999 per year after the first operating year unless renewal is declined under the accepted terms. Optional annual compliance support is separately scoped and accepted.

Architecture qualification and jurisdiction

Qualification produces one of three preliminary routes: architecture supported, professional review required, or architecture not supported. The Pro Alpha product flow opens checkout only for a supported path. A supported route does not itself establish Trader Tax Status, election validity, tax treatment, or a final professional conclusion.

Accepted files receive the complete two-entity commercial scope; the product is not reduced into a cheaper tier. Residence, where the business operates, foreign-qualification exposure, state tax and recurring-report obligations, existing entities, ownership, capital source, and other facts can change the approved filing jurisdictions. No state is selected solely by the public headline.

Not legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice

Neither Pro Alpha Systems nor Pro Alpha OS is a law firm, CPA firm, accounting firm, broker-dealer, investment adviser, bank, exchange, or government agency. Software prepares and routes records. Applicable professionals review facts-specific legal or tax work when required by the accepted engagement, and authorized filing channels submit approved actions.

Entity choice, Trader Tax Status, Section 475, S-election eligibility and timing, reasonable compensation, payroll, benefits, QBI, PTET, depreciation, accountable plans, foreign qualification, and tax treatment depend on the customer’s actual facts and applicable law. Customers remain responsible for complete and accurate information and for client-owned deadlines identified in the implementation file.

Automation and human review

Automation controls preliminary qualification, payment-state reconciliation, detailed intake, document assembly, dependency sequencing, private filing handoffs, status updates, and record delivery. It can support, hold, or decline an architecture. Automation does not convert an unresolved facts-and-circumstances determination into a guaranteed conclusion.

Irreversible filing and payment actions remain behind completeness, authority, conflict, duplicate-prevention, and human-review gates. A workflow may pause when data is inconsistent, a service is unavailable, authorization is incomplete, a cost exceeds an approved ceiling, or a professional review is required.

What is and is not guaranteed

For the agreed scope, Pro Alpha Systems uses Pro Alpha OS to assemble the stated implementation packet and check generated records against information the customer supplied. If a material discrepancy is identified before submission, the file is held for correction rather than knowingly submitted with conflicting data.

Pro Alpha OS does not guarantee Trader Tax Status, a Section 475 or S-election outcome, IRS or state treatment, tax savings, refund amounts, filing speed controlled by a third party, name availability, bank or exchange approval, credit or financing approval, brokerage access, anonymity, or any trading or investment result.

Privacy and data handling

The pre-qualification flow stores the desk request in the current browser session so the customer can move into the commercial flow. It does not request SSNs, tax IDs, bank credentials, exchange passwords, or payment-card numbers. Clearing the browser session removes that local request.

Stripe processes checkout, payment credentials, and the customer information collected on the hosted payment page under Stripe’s own terms and privacy practices. After a settled payment is verified, the controlled filing intake receives the entity names, contact information, principal-office address, requested timing, ownership flags, acknowledgements, and other purpose-limited implementation data the customer submits.

Implementation data may be processed by purpose-limited infrastructure, payment, filing, registered-agent, document, communications, security, and professional-service providers as needed to deliver the accepted scope. Provider access is limited to operational need and applicable contractual or legal requirements. Pro Alpha Systems does not ask customers to place sensitive credentials in AI prompts and does not represent customer filing data as public model-training material.

Public records, ownership, and identity

Government formation records can require and publish entity names, filing dates, registered-agent names or addresses, organizers, officers, managers, or other information required by the filing jurisdiction. Pro Alpha Systems cannot lawfully conceal, falsify, or override required public-record content.

Public-record privacy is not anonymity from banks, exchanges, tax authorities, courts, regulators, counterparties, or other institutions with lawful identity and ownership/control requirements. The Ownership & KYC Record is designed for consistency and operational readiness—not evasion of disclosure obligations and not a claim that a domestic U.S. entity has a federal BOI filing obligation.

Payment, government fees, and refunds

The fixed year-one desk price is collected through Stripe-hosted checkout under the merchant name Crestside Consultants LLC. Payment-method availability, installment products, settlement timing, disputes, chargebacks, and financing eligibility are controlled by the payment provider and do not change the accepted Pro Alpha OS scope.

If formation has not been submitted to a state, a refund request made within 5 business days may be approved minus payment-processing charges and nonrecoverable government or third-party costs already incurred for the customer’s file. After a state submission or another irreversible, authorized filing action, the associated implementation and filing fees are earned to the extent permitted by the accepted engagement terms and applicable law.

A failed, delayed, or rejected filing is handled according to the cause, recoverability of external fees, and the accepted scope. A chargeback does not cancel government filings or other irreversible actions already authorized and taken.

AI-assisted drafting disclosure

Document drafts, routing suggestions, completeness checks, and onboarding guidance may use automated systems or large language models. Generated output is not itself a filed election, accepted formation, legal opinion, tax return, or professional sign-off.

Customers must review requested names, ownership, addresses, timing, purpose, and acknowledgements before submitting the detailed intake. Applicable final actions remain subject to the controls described above.

Customer responsibilities

The customer must provide accurate, current, and complete facts; promptly resolve action items; avoid using an unaccepted entity as though it were active; preserve trading and business evidence; and obtain independent advice where a matter falls outside the software and coordination scope.

The customer must not submit another person’s information without authority, use the system for unlawful concealment or evasion, provide false filing data, attempt to bypass review controls, or place SSNs, passwords, card numbers, private keys, or account credentials into general text fields.

Do not treat a generated record as an accepted filing.

The implementation desk identifies when a formation or filing has been submitted, accepted, held, or returned. Until acceptance is confirmed, do not trade under, retitle accounts to, or represent the proposed entity as active.
