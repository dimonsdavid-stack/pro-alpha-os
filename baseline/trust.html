Data handling

Minimum necessary data by stage.

Qualification: trading profile, residence, existing-entity/election flags, contact details, and routing outcome stored in the current browser session.

Checkout: payment credentials and checkout identity processed by Stripe under its own terms.

Paid implementation: approved entity names/jurisdictions, ownership flags, principal-office record, authorization, filing state, and purpose-limited workflow evidence.

External execution: only information required for the authorized filing, registered-agent, document, payment, or professional-review action.

Subprocessor categories

Operational need defines access.

  • Cloud hosting and workflow
  • Hosted payment processing
  • Filing and registered-agent operations
  • Document generation and storage
  • Customer communications
  • Professional review and advisory

Government records may publish registered-agent, organizer, officer, manager, address, or other jurisdiction-required information. Provider isolation in the application is not a promise of anonymity from public records or lawful KYC.

Regulatory logic provenance

Sources and rule versions belong in the system.

IRS · trader facts

Trader status remains facts-and-circumstances based.

Holding periods, trade frequency and amount, livelihood intent, time devoted, substantiality, continuity, and regularity matter; this intake does not determine the result.

FinCEN · BOI logic

Domestic U.S. companies are exempt from federal BOI reporting under the August 2026 final rule.

The product maintains an Ownership & KYC Record for banks, brokers, exchanges, counterparties, and operating consistency; it does not market that record as a domestic federal BOI filing obligation.
