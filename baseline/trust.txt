Hosted payment boundary
Payment-card credentials stay on Stripe-hosted checkout. The application receives only the resulting payment state and authorized implementation data.
Purpose-limited intake
Pre-qualification does not request SSNs, tax IDs, bank credentials, exchange passwords, card numbers, or private keys.
Irreversible-action gates
Submission is blocked behind completeness, authority, duplicate, cost-ceiling, and explicit human-approval checks.
Reference-linked reconciliation
Payment, intake, workflow state, external status, and accepted records remain attached to one implementation reference.
Provider isolation
